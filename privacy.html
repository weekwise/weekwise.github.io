Privacy Policy

Weekwise: GLP-1 Tracker · Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: everything you log in Weekwise stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We never see, sell or share your health information.

What you enter and where it is stored

Weekwise lets you log medication doses, injection sites, weight, waist size, side effects, food, protein, fiber, water and your goals. All of it is stored only in a database on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server, and we have no way to access it.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you saved a backup.

Backups and reports you choose to share

You can export a backup file or a PDF report for your doctor. These files are created on your device and go only where you send them (for example iCloud Drive, Files, Google Drive or an email you write). We never receive them.

Reminders

Dose and refill reminders are scheduled on your device using your phone's own notification system. No reminder content is sent to us.

Subscriptions

Weekwise Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle payment; we never see your name, card or payment details.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID created on your device, your purchase history for Weekwise, and basic device information (such as the app version and country of your store account). This is not linked to your name or email and is used only to unlock Pro and to count subscriptions. RevenueCat never receives anything you log in the app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up, and no email or phone number collected.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Children

Weekwise is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry in the app at any time, or delete everything by deleting the app. Because your logs never leave your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will request deletion.

Not medical advice

Weekwise is a personal tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnose or treat any condition. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
