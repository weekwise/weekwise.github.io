Privacy Policy

Vialwise: Peptide Log · Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: everything you log in Vialwise stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no ads inside the app. We never see, sell or share what you log.

What you enter and where it is stored

Vialwise lets you log compounds, doses, units, schedules, injection sites, vial counts, weight and side effects. All of it is stored only in a database on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server, and we have no way to access it.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you saved a backup.

Backups and reports you choose to share

You can export a backup file or a PDF report. These files are created on your device and go only where you send them (for example iCloud Drive, Files or an email you write). We never receive them.

Reminders

Dose and vial reminders are scheduled on your device using your phone's own notification system. No reminder content is sent to us.

Subscriptions

Vialwise Pro is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, card or payment details.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID created on your device, your purchase history for Vialwise, and basic device information (such as the app version and the country of your store account). This is not linked to your name or email and is used only to unlock Pro and to count subscriptions. RevenueCat never receives anything you log in the app. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

The app also uses Apple's own attribution system (AdServices) to tell RevenueCat which Apple Search Ads campaign, if any, led to the install. Apple provides this without any tracking permission and without identifying you.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up, and no email or phone number collected.
  • No ads shown inside the app, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share anything you log.

Children

Vialwise is meant for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry in the app at any time, or delete everything by deleting the app. Because your logs never leave your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will request deletion.

Not medical advice

Vialwise is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice, recommend doses or products, or diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version adds ad measurement, this page will describe it before that version is released.
