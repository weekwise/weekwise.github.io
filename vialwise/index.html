Vialwise: Peptide Log

Vialwise is a simple log for your injections: doses, schedules, injection sites, vial counts, weight and side effects, with reminders and a PDF report you can share with your provider.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Use Settings → Back up to save a copy to iCloud Drive or Files, and Restore to bring it back.

Reminders aren't arriving. Check that notifications are allowed for Vialwise in your iPhone Settings, and that a reminder time is set for the compound.

How do I cancel Pro? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vialwise.

Does Vialwise recommend doses? No. It records what you enter and never suggests doses, products or schedules.
